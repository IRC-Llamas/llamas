# About ##llamas

We are llamas. We're a social channel with a fun environment, no fixed topic, and a community of friendly regulars.

`##llamas` has grown from being fairly small channel to one of the more active ones on Libera.Chat.
To keep the channel enjoyable, we (the `##llamas` chanops) have a few rules that people are expected to follow.
Further down, there's also some stuff about how the channel operates.

## The Channel Atmosphere We Want

We want `##llamas` to be broadly enjoyable, to provide a place where people can socialize in good faith.
We have no interest fitting any specific viewpoint, narrative, or other agenda.
We want a channel where people generally get along and can unwind.
Users of `##llamas` should make an effort to be friendly towards others,
and be able to reasonably trust that others are reciprocating the effort.
Even during debates or arguments, we should be able to trust that everyone is arguing in good faith.

## Rules

The main rule is simple on paper:

> Don't significantly degrade the channel atmosphere.

This is a very broad and loosely-defined rule, and that's that way for a reason.
The chanops cannot foresee every possible behavior that someone might take
to make the channel worse for others, and while the chanops are generally permissive,
sometimes it's necessary to apply discretion.

That said, there are some specific behaviors that have been generally accepted
as degrading the atmosphere, so here are those in more detail:

### Violations of Network Policy

`##llamas`, like all channels on Libera.Chat, is bound by
[the network's policies](https://libera.chat/policies#unwanted-content-and-behaviour).
The local chanops are proactive in enforcing them. In particular, the following are strictly forbidden
and will result in a swift removal from the channel and/or a referral to Libera.Chat staff:

* Ban or quiet evasion.
* In no particular order: antisemitism, homophobia, racism, sexism, or transphobia.
* More generally: speech that explicitly degrades any particular demographic or incites violence.
* Threatening or attempting to harm someone.
* Doxing.
* Posting or linking sexual content.

### Bad Faith

`##llamas` participants are expected to act in good faith.
We do not tolerate trolling, saying things that you know aren't true,
or misrepresenting your opinions to try to influence others.
In particular, `##llamas`'s chanops may disregard claims of attempted humor
as a defense of bad behavior, as some people regularly make them in bad faith.

### Soapboxing

`##llamas` is not your soapbox!
Please be mindful that excessive chatter from you does not deprive others
of the opportunity to speak. This is also not a space that welcomes
attempts to preach to others about your viewpoint.

Relatedly, `##llamas` is also neither your online battle arena nor your group therapy center.
Please do not bring interpersonal drama from other places to `##llamas`
or use `##llamas` as a place solely for wallowing in misery.
Both these things really drag down the channel atmosphere.

### Violating Boundaries

`##llamas` is at its best when people don't have to be defensive because someone else is needling them.
If someone expresses a personal boundary with regards to how they want to be treated by others,
please consider honoring it. Chanops may intervene if someone refuses to stop:

- Insulting or otherwise being needlessly hostile to someone else.
- Asking someone else probing personal questions.
- Calling someone else nicknames other than the nickname(s) that someone uses for themself.
- Spreading falsehoods about someone else.
- Misgendering someone else, regardless of if that someone is trans or not.

### Violating Privacy

`##llamas` believes that people who aren't public figures have a reasonable right to privacy.
In general, you may not disclose personal information about someone that they have neither disclosed themselves in `##llamas` nor verifiably agreed to being disclosed.

Additionally, speaking for someone else about themselves is discouraged.
If you repeat previously-shared personal information about someone else (without their agreement in advance)
and it falls into any of the below categories, you will be considered to have violated their privacy:

- Their real/legal name(s).
- Their age.
- Any information about their location of residence more specific than their country.
- Any pictures of them.
- Anything else that could be condered "dox" or could harm the subject if widely known.
- Anything that they previously requested not to be repeated _or_ later object (within a reasonable timeframe) to having been repeated.

All of the above is in addition to Libera.Chat policies on doxing.

### Poor Handling of Contentious Topics

Users **may** discuss current events and political topics.

However! As stated above, `##llamas` is not your soapbox!
If you engage in political discussion,
you will be expected to listen to what others are saying in response
and not disregard critical examination of your ideas.

Similarly, you may not repeatedly disrupt active conversations to talk about news or politics.
Yes, these things can be important, but they rarely take priority over everything else.

We also expect users to do their due diligence when they discuss things
and not spread proven falsehoods. If your pet conspiracy theory has counterarguments
that you're not going to address, don't bring it to `##llamas`.

Finally, don't get mean with other people just for disagreeing with you.
If you find yourself getting heated to the point that you want to start attacking people,
consider stepping away for a few minutes.
Discussions are not something to "win" or "lose", and withdrawing is not a concession.

### Immaturity

`##llamas` expects its participants to display a level of maturity
that one would expect from a responsible adult in a typical in-person casual setting.
Tantrums, name-calling, excessive crude humor, or blatant attention-seeking may result in a time-out.

`##llamas` **allows** profanity, but don't overdo it.

### Noise

It should go without saying,
but `##llamas` is for humans to chat by exchanging text messages.
This means it is not for ASCII art or text dumps. Please take those somewhere that wants them.
Our bots may auto-kick for flooding.

Please also avoid regularly using colored text.
`##llamas` is one of the few major social channels that does not strip out formatting codes.
It would be nice to keep it that way.

### Bot Abuse

First of all, __don't bring your bots to `##llamas`!__
We will kickban bots we didn't specifically allow in the channel and may refer them to Libera.Chat staff.

Bot abuse is using a bot in a way that is not intended (as determined by the bot's owner)
_and_ that negatively impacts the bot's owner or anyone in the channel.
Examples of bot abuse include:

- Using a bot in a way that causes spam.
- Using bot commands to circumvent ignores, quiets, or bans.
- Spamming the bots with messages, including in PM, especially if doing so may exhaust API request quotas.
- Exploiting any bugs in the bots.

## About The Chanops

You can use `/msg chanserv flags ##llamas +o` to view a list of `##llamas` channel operators.
`/ignore` them at your own risk!

### Contacting Ops

If you feel you are being treated unfairly by others,
or are bothered by the conduct of others to the point that is causing you to not have an enjoyable experience,
or have concerns about the rules,
come visit `##llamas-ops`.
As is common for `-ops` channels, we ask that people not idle there and may kick idlers.

If op attention is required immediately (e.g. due to active spamming),
voiced users can say `!ops` in `##llamas` to notify the chanops that their attention is required.

### Expectations For Ops

Channel operators in `##llamas` generally seek consensus among active chanops before taking action,
and if that fails, put proposed actions to a vote.
This can mean that we're sometimes slow to respond, but it helps keep things fair when there are personality conflicts.

Typically, the escalation for repeated rule violations is:
warning, removal of autovoice (if applicable, see below), short quiet, longer quiet, then long-term ban.
The chanops may skip parts of this progression for particularly severe violations.
If you are quieted or banned, you can typically visit `##llamas-ops` to learn how long the ban will last and/or to appeal it.
If you are banned from `##llamas-ops`, you have damaged the relationship between yourself and the chanops beyond repair,
and there exists no possibility of you appealing your ban
(Libera.Chat staff do not get involved in strictly channel-level matters).

`##llamas` chanops are bound by the same rules as everyone else in the channel.
If you have an issue with one, visiting `##llamas-ops` is still recommended.
If a `##llamas` chanop violates network policy, contact Libera.Chat staff.

## Autovoice

As with most public spaces on any chat platform, `##llamas` attracts idlers.
To recognize participation, one of our bots (`toy`)
automatically voices people who are in good standing and have been active for a significant amount of time.

To be autovoiced, you must have a [Libera.Chat account](https://libera.chat/guides/registration)
and have 7 days (that's 168 hours) of active time in the channel.
Active time is tracked by `toy` as "time wasted". You can view your own stats with the `!stat` command.
Active time increases as you send consecutive messages no more than a certain (undisclosed) period of time apart from each other;
occasionally dropping single messages into the channel will not increase your active time.

Once you have been active for long enough,
`toy` will automatically voice you when you send a message without being voiced.

If you wish to opt out of autovoice, you can use the `!autovoice` command.
